01 — About

Analyst by trade, educator by habit.

I'm an experienced analyst with a strong skill set in R, Python, SQL, Microsoft Fabric, Power BI and Tableau, along with working knowledge of cloud architecture.

What I enjoy most is the moment a dataset stops being a spreadsheet and starts telling a story — the dashboard a leadership team opens every Monday, or the chart that settles a debate in one glance.

I'm also a passionate advocate for data literacy. I love helping executives, analysts, consultants and professionals on their data upskilling journey, and to date I've onboarded more than 500 professionals onto SQL, Power BI and Tableau.

What I can help with

  • Designing dashboards & reporting in Power BI and Tableau
  • SQL, Python & R analysis — from raw data to insight
  • Modern data platforms with Microsoft Fabric & the cloud
  • Hands-on data training for teams and leaders
