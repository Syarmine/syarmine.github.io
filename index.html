Approach

How I work. Four commitments.

  1. 01

    Start from the decision, not the dataset.

    Analysis earns its place by changing what someone does next. Begin with the choice on the table and work back to the evidence it needs.

    Decisions
  2. 02

    Look at the system before the fix.

    Most failures sit between organisations, budgets and incentives, not inside a single tool. Understand the system a problem lives in before proposing a solution to it.

    Systems
  3. 03

    State the uncertainty.

    Good evidence comes with error bars. Saying what we do not know is part of the analysis, not a footnote to it.

    Evidence
  4. 04

    Build capability, not dependency.

    The test of any project is whether the institution can carry it on afterwards. Teaching and documentation are part of the delivery.

    Capability
